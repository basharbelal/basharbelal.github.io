About

Bashar Belal is a Jordanian interdisciplinary creative whose work moves beyond a single medium or discipline. With a background in interior architecture and design, he brings together different perspectives into a singular creative vision. Driven by curiosity, identity and culture, his ideas take shape through imagery, fashion, spaces and experiences. Sometimes a creative project, sometimes an entire brand.

  • Creative Direction / Art Direction
  • Brand Strategy / Campaign Development
  • Film / Production
  • Events / Experiences
  • Social Media Strategy / Influencer Strategies
